<p>Using git.wtf means you accept these few terms. They are short because the site is.</p>
<h2>The old posts</h2>
<p>The archive's posts were written to help, and the commands in them are given as they are. Try them on a copy first. Git does exactly what you ask, which is not always what you meant, and we cannot be responsible for what a command does to your work.</p>
<h2>The incidents</h2>
<p>Each incident is a retelling of a public report, with a link to its source, told as parody and commentary. If you are in one and think we got something wrong, <a href="https://github.com/meSingh/git.wtf/issues">open an issue</a> and we will look at it.</p>
<h2>Sharing</h2>
<p>Share the incidents, the episodes and the images as much as you like, with a link back to git.wtf.</p>
<h2>Names</h2>
<p>GitHub, X, LinkedIn, Instagram, YouTube and the tools named in incidents belong to their owners. Naming them here is not an endorsement, and they do not endorse us.</p>
<h2>Changes</h2>
<p>These terms may change; this page always has the current ones. Last updated 8 November 2026.</p>
